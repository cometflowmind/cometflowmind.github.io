Privacy policy

Last updated: 16 November 2026

In short: everything you put into Cometflow Mind stays on your phone.

  • The app has no internet access. On Android it doesn't even ask for permission to use the internet.
  • There's no account, no sign-in, no analytics, no ads and no tracking.
  • The AI that turns your brain dumps into tasks runs on your phone.
  • We, the developer, never receive anything you write, and have no server to receive it on.

This policy covers the Cometflow Mind app for Android and iPhone. "We" means the app's developer, an independent developer in Canada.

What the app stores, and where

The app keeps the following on your phone, in its own private storage:

  • your brain dumps, the tasks made from them, your notes, and your settings;
  • an Activity log of what the app did, such as "a brain dump was processed" or "a backup was saved", with counts and error messages. It never contains what you wrote. Entries older than 90 days are deleted automatically.

None of this leaves your phone unless you move it yourself, with a backup (see below). The app's data is also left out of your phone's own cloud backup (Google's backup on Android, iCloud backup on iPhone), so it isn't copied to those services either.

The on-device AI

Cometflow Mind uses the AI model built into your phone: Gemini Nano on supported Android phones, and Apple's on-device model on supported iPhones. Your text is processed on the phone and isn't sent anywhere. The model itself is installed and updated by your phone's operating system, not by the app.

Speak privately (dictation)

The Speak privately button uses your phone's on-device speech recognition, so your voice is turned into text on the phone. If a language isn't installed yet, the app can ask your phone to download it. Your phone's operating system does that download; the app never connects to anything.

Your keyboard's own microphone button is part of your keyboard, not of this app. Depending on its settings, it may send your voice to the keyboard maker's servers.

The app asks for microphone access only when you first tap Speak privately, and listens only while it shows that it's listening.

Backups

You can save a backup file, or let the app save automatic backups to a folder you choose. A backup goes only where you put it, for example your phone's storage or a cloud drive you pick. Once it's there, that place's own privacy terms apply.

Backups can be protected with a password. They're then encrypted (AES-256) before they're saved. The password for automatic backups is kept in your phone's secure storage (Android Keystore, or the iPhone's Keychain). We can't recover a forgotten password.

Purchases

Some features are unlocked with a one-time purchase. Google Play or the App Store handles the payment. They tell the app only whether your account owns the unlock. We never see your name, email address or payment details from the purchase. The stores' own privacy policies apply to the payment.

Permissions the app asks for

  • Notifications, to remind you of tasks at the time you set.
  • Alarms and reminders (Android), so reminders arrive on time.
  • Microphone, only for Speak privately.
  • Speech recognition (iPhone), only for Speak privately.
  • Face ID, Touch ID, fingerprint or your screen lock, only if you turn on the app lock. The check is done by your phone; the app never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN.

You can turn any of these off in your phone's settings. The rest of the app keeps working.

What we receive

Nothing, unless you contact us. If you email support, we receive your email address and whatever you choose to write. We use it only to answer you, and never share or sell it. You can ask us at any time to delete your messages.

Deleting your data

You can delete any brain dump, task or note in the app. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored on your phone. Backup files you saved elsewhere stay where you put them until you delete them.

Children

The app isn't directed at children, and it collects no data from anyone, children included.

Your rights

Because we don't collect personal information through the app, we hold none of yours to access, correct or delete, apart from any emails you send us. Canada's privacy law (PIPEDA) and laws elsewhere, such as the GDPR, give you rights over personal information. Write to us to use them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. If the app ever started collecting or sending data, we would say so here and in the app before it happened.

Contact

Questions about privacy: cometflowmind.support@gmail.com
