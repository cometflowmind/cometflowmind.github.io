Getting started

Which phones does it work on?

Turning brain dumps into tasks uses the AI model built into your phone, so it needs:

  • Android: a phone that supports Google's on-device Gemini Nano (for example, recent Pixel and Samsung Galaxy flagships);
  • iPhone: an iPhone that supports Apple Intelligence, with Apple Intelligence turned on in Settings.

On other phones the app still saves your brain dumps, and you can split each one into tasks yourself with Split it myself. Tasks, reminders and everything else work the same.

It says the AI model is downloading

The first time, your phone downloads its on-device model. Your phone does this, not the app, and it can take a while, often longest on Wi-Fi while charging. Your brain dumps wait in a queue and are processed once it's ready.

Why does processing only happen with the app open?

Phones only let the on-device AI run for the app on screen. Brain dumps you write wait and are processed the next time you open the app.

Why do I have to review the suggested tasks?

The AI's tasks are suggestions until you accept them. You can edit, remove or add tasks before they go into your list, so nothing ends up there that you didn't mean.

Reminders

My reminders arrive late or not at all
  • Make sure notifications are on for Cometflow Mind in your phone's settings.
  • Android: allow Alarms & reminders for the app (Settings → Apps → Cometflow Mind). Without it, Android may deliver a reminder up to an hour late, and the app says so under the reminder.
  • Battery savers can hold notifications back. If yours does, set the app's battery use to "Unrestricted".

Speak privately (dictation)

How is it different from my keyboard's microphone?

Speak privately uses your phone's on-device speech recognition, so your voice never leaves the phone. Your keyboard's microphone belongs to the keyboard and may send your voice to its maker's servers.

It doesn't understand one of my languages

Speak privately can hear any of your phone's languages whose offline speech is installed. If one isn't, the app names it under the button with a Download button. You can also add languages yourself.

  • Android: Settings → System → Languages → Speech → On-device recognition.
  • iPhone: Speak privately listens in your iPhone's language (Settings → General → Language & Region), where the iPhone supports on-device dictation for it.

Backups and moving to a new phone

How do I back up my brain dumps and tasks?

Open the ⋮ menu → Backup & restore → Export backup, and save the file somewhere safe, such as a cloud drive. You can protect it with a password, and it's then encrypted. With the unlock, the app can also save automatic backups to a folder you choose.

Your phone's own cloud backup deliberately leaves the app's data out, so a backup file is the only copy outside your phone.

How do I move everything to a new phone?

On the old phone, export a backup and save it somewhere the new phone can reach. On the new phone, install the app, then ⋮ menu → Backup & restore → Restore from file.

I forgot my backup password

We're sorry, but it can't be recovered. The file is encrypted with it, and we never see it. Save a new backup with a password you'll remember.

App lock

How does the app lock work?

Turn it on in ⋮ menu → Privacy. The app then asks for your fingerprint, face or screen lock when you return to it after a minute away. Your phone does the check, and the app never sees your fingerprint, face or PIN. If biometrics fail, your phone's PIN, pattern or password always works.

The unlock (purchase)

What does the unlock add?

A one-time purchase, kept for good, with no subscription. It adds Your week (a weekly reflection), Ask about your notes, Break it down and Automatic backups. Everything else is free.

I bought it but it's locked on my new phone

Sign in to the same Google Play or Apple account you bought it with, open any locked feature, and tap Restore purchase. A purchase on Google Play doesn't carry over to an iPhone, or the other way around.

How do I get a refund?

The store handles payments and refunds, so request one from Google Play (play.google.com → Order history) or Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com). If a refund is granted, the paid features lock again; your data stays.

Privacy
